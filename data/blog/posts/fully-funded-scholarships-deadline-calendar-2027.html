The big programmes: how their timing works

These programmes are among the most searched. Their dates depend on your country or the course you choose, which is why they are not in the table above. What is below comes from the funders' own pages as recorded in our directory.

Chevening (UK)

Fully funded one-year master's degrees in the UK for emerging leaders. Applications for the 2027/28 academic year opened in August 2026 and close in October 2026; the exact date depends on your country, so this round may already have closed. Read the Chevening listing for the official timeline and plan for the next round: this year's opened in August.

Commonwealth Shared Scholarships (UK)

Closing date: 8 December 2026 at 16:00 GMT, for master's courses starting in September or October 2027, for candidates from listed Commonwealth countries. See the listing.

Gates Cambridge (UK)

The round for applicants outside the United States has two possible deadlines: 8 December 2026 or 6 January 2027 (11:59 pm UK time), depending on the funding deadline of your Cambridge course. Check your course in the Cambridge directory. See the listing.

Eiffel Excellence Scholarship (France)

Campus France must receive the application by 6 January 2027. You cannot apply yourself: a French university or school has to nominate you, so contact your chosen institution well before the end of 2026. See the listing.

Erasmus Mundus Joint Masters (Europe)

There is no single deadline: each master's programme sets its own, and in most cases applications open between October and January for courses starting the following academic year. Browse the official catalogue and list the deadlines of the three or four programmes you like. See the listing.

DAAD (Germany)

DAAD publishes a database of 148 funding options when we last read it, each with its own deadline in its details page. Filter by the country you live in and your study level, then note each deadline. See the listing.

MEXT (Japan)

The Embassy Recommendation route is run by the Japanese embassy in your country. For students arriving in Japan in 2027, the official guidelines say recruitment took place between April and May of the year before, with first screening between May and July; the schedule differs by nationality. If the 2028 arrival round follows the same pattern, spring 2027 is the time to watch your embassy's page, but that is a pattern, not an announcement. See the listing.

Global Korea Scholarship (GKS)

The undergraduate embassy track for 2027 closed at the end of September 2026 in the countries whose notices we read. University tracks set their own dates, and the graduate call for 2027 had not been announced when we checked. See the listing.

T?rkiye Burslari, Stipendium Hungaricum and Australia Awards

These three have closed for this year, which is useful to know when you plan: the 2026 T?rkiye Burslari call ran from 10 January to 20 February 2026, the 2026/27 Stipendium Hungaricum call closed on 15 January 2026, and Australia Awards for study in 2027 closed on 30 April 2026. The next calls have not been published when we checked, so the first months of 2027 are the time to start watching: see the T?rkiye Burslari, Stipendium Hungaricum and Australia Awards listings.

Fulbright (United States)

Applications go to the Fulbright commission or the US embassy in your home country, and each sets its own deadline. See the listing.

How to use this calendar

  1. Work backwards. Take the deadline, subtract two months, and that is the date by which your documents should be ready. Transcripts, notarised copies and recommendation letters are what usually run late.
  2. Check your nationality. Many scholarships list the countries they accept. In the scholarship directory, choose your nationality and the directory shows only calls open to you.
  3. Apply through the official link. Every SAS listing links to the funder's own application page. Do not pay anyone to be "considered" for a scholarship; if a website asks for money, check the funder's own page first.
  4. Keep a spreadsheet. Scholarship, deadline, documents needed, who writes your letters, status. It takes ten minutes and prevents missed dates.
  5. Do not rely on one application. Even strong candidates are not selected every time. Apply to several that really fit you.
