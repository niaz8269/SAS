# Keywords for SAS (2026-11-12)

## How this list was made, and its limits
- 125 phrases, chosen for relevance to scholarships and studying abroad, for what the site really has data for (117 published scholarships, 48 destination guides, 308 China universities, services) and for how people word the question.
- **Search volumes were not measured.** The Ahrefs plan available here has no usage units and Semrush reported "no API units", so there is no volume, difficulty or ranking number in this file. The list is ordered by topic, not by popularity. Search Console (Performance > Queries) shows which of these phrases already bring impressions: use it to re-order the list after two to four weeks.
- A phrase is the **main phrase of one page type** (so pages do not compete) and appears naturally elsewhere. No page repeats a phrase to rank: every added sentence is a stored fact.
- Intent: **I** information, **C** compare, **T** take action (apply or ask).

## What the code now does with them (all from stored data)
- **Scholarship pages**: an "At a glance" paragraph and "Quick answers" (deadline, who can apply, required documents, is it fully funded, where the information comes from) with matching FAQ markup; links to the destination guide and the landing pages it belongs to; six related scholarships. A statement appears only if its data is stored. Funding is never called "fully funded" unless it was verified.
- **Landing pages**: 12 destination pages, 5 degree pages and the funding pages that have 3 or more published scholarships. They are in the sitemap, the footer ("Popular scholarship searches") and `/site-map`, and disappear if scholarships are unpublished.
- **Destination guides** link to their landing page; **university pages** carry the data summary and links to nearby universities.

### Why there is no "fully funded scholarships" page yet
31 published scholarships have funding type "full", but none is marked as verified fully funded (`fully_funded_verified`). By the site's own rule (DIR-04) they are not labelled "Fully funded", so the page `/scholarships/fully-funded` does not exist and no text claims it. When staff verify the covered costs of a call in the Admin, that call is labelled, and the page appears automatically once 3 calls are verified. That is the single highest-value data task for the phrase "fully funded scholarships".

## Real search demand check (13 November 2026)
**Method.** Search volumes cannot be measured here (Ahrefs: 0 units; Semrush: no units). Instead, 1,238 queries were sent to Google's public search-suggestion service (the list that drops down as people type), in five countries (US, India, Pakistan, Nigeria, Bangladesh), starting from 60 topic phrases and, for the 8 core ones, every letter a to z. That returned 3,092 distinct phrases. Google orders suggestions by how often people search, so a phrase's score here is *how high and how often it appears*: a relative demand signal, **not a monthly volume**. The 60 phrases used as starting points appear artificially high; the expansions are the real finding.

**What this changes**
1. **The biggest demand is a question the site can answer from its own data**: "chinese government scholarship requirements / how to get it / deadline / rules / benefits". The site holds 4 published Chinese Government Scholarship calls (type B, Silk Road and others) with documents, funding components and deadlines. One page that sets them side by side (the documents that recur, the benefits, the next confirmed deadlines, the route) would match these searches with real data. Not built yet.
2. **Field pages are missing.** "Engineering / medical / MBA / undergraduate scholarships for international students" are among the most-shown phrases. The data supports field pages today: Engineering 14 scholarships, Natural Sciences 14, Social Sciences 12, Business and Economics 10 (MBA), Agriculture and Environment 9, Medicine and Health 8, Computer Science 7, Humanities and Arts 6, Law 5. The degree pages already exist; subject pages do not.
3. **"From my country" pages are missing.** People add their country ("from nigeria / pakistan / philippines / kenya / india"). The data lists accepted nationalities per scholarship (Kenya 25, Tanzania 25, Nigeria 23, Bangladesh 22, India 19, Ghana 20 ... explicit lists), plus 43 scholarships open to any nationality, so pages for the top nationalities would each differ from the others. Not built yet.
4. **The 125-phrase list was right on topic and missing these shapes**: "requirements / rules / last date / benefits / application status" for CSC, "can i study in china (for free)", "how to apply ... from <country>", and "in <country>" combinations for each field. They are added to the list below.
5. **The "how to" guides in the outlines are justified** by the demand for "how to write a motivation letter" (strong in all five countries), "how to study abroad for free" and "how much does it cost to study abroad".
